Gibbs' Reflective Cycle

1. Description

In this module, I completed an essay assignment that required me to write in academic English and apply Harvard referencing. I also explored the learning platform and the available support services, using the recommended articles on essay structure and academic style as guidance.

2. Feelings

At first, I found the assignment challenging, especially because it required adapting to academic English and being meticulous with referencing. The emphasis on referencing everything was new to me compared with common practices in Denmark or the US, and I initially felt uncertain about doing it correctly.

3. Evaluation

However, once I started applying the guidance from the articles, the process became easier. Having clear resources on essay structure and style made it much simpler to get started, and I quickly found that academic writing felt less intimidating than I had expected.

4. Analysis

Looking back, I can see that the main difficulty was not the content itself but my unfamiliarity with the conventions of academic English and referencing. By following structured examples and revisiting the articles when needed, I was able to turn what initially felt like a barrier into a learning opportunity that strengthened my understanding of academic expectations.

5. Conclusion

Overall, the module helped me build confidence in using academic English and Harvard referencing. I now have a clearer sense of how to plan, structure, and support my written work, and I feel better prepared for future assignments on the programme.

6. Action plan

Going forward, I will make a habit of consulting the essay structure and referencing resources at the start of each new written assignment. I will also continue to refine my writing style by practising academic English regularly and seeking feedback whenever possible, so that these skills become a natural part of my work.
